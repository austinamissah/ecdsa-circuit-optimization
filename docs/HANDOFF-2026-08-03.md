## What was mid-way through

**A seed grind at delta 2** — `scratchpad/lam/grind.sh`, running in the background at the time of
writing, results accumulating in `scratchpad/lam/grind/`. Stage 1 screens nonces in ladder mode;
every classical-clean hit goes to the real harness in stage 2. It claims a seed only on
0 classical / 0 phase / 0 ancilla. **Nothing about it was confirmed before the session ended — treat
any output as unverified until re-checked.** Round 1 (4,000 nonces from base 170000000000000) had
not finished its screening pass.

One thing learned while setting it up, worth knowing before re-running: **the shot ladder's saving
collapses at low λ.** At λ_classical = 5.79 roughly 19% of nonces reach the full 9,024-shot rung,
so expected shots per nonce is ~6,100 rather than the ~1,255 the ladder gives at λ = 20. The ladder
was designed for a high-λ regime; at delta 2 it saves ~33%, not 7×.

## Grind round 1 completed after the note was written

4,000 nonces screened at delta 2 → **12 classical-clean candidates** against 12.2 predicted, and
**12/12 came back `classical = 0` on the real harness** — the screen's classical channel is exact
in the field, not just on the 199-nonce gate.

**No seed yet, and one number needs correcting.** Phase counts on those 12 were
`1 2 2 2 2 2 3 3 4 4 5 6`, so λ_phase_only measured *conditionally on classical-clean* is
**3.000 ± 0.426**, not the 1.90 obtained by subtracting λ_classical from the covariance estimate of
λ_total. This conditional figure is the directly-measured one and should be preferred. Consequences:

- **P(phase-clean | classical-clean) = e^-3.0 = 5.0%, so ~20 candidates per seed, not ~7.**
- λ_total ≈ 5.787 + 3.000 = **8.79**, against 8.111 from the covariance estimator (n=42). The two
  agree inside their uncertainties; treat λ_total as "about 8–9".
- A seed costs ~6,500 nonces screened plus ~20 harness confirmations — roughly **an hour**, on the
  round-1 rate. Feasibility is unchanged; only the constant moved.

0 phase-clean in 12 has probability 0.85^12 = 0.14 under the old figure and 0.95^12 = 0.54 under the
corrected one, which is itself mild evidence the corrected figure is right.

## Standing hazards confirmed this session

- **Hold `SUB4_APPLY_STRIP=0` in every arm of any λ or structural experiment.** The strip repoints
  under any stream edit and you will measure its corruption instead of your knob. This is what made
  the mod-3 rule look true.
- The `avgT`/qubits/score of a *failing* config are still readable from `results.tsv`, which
  `eval_circuit` appends on its FAIL path — useful for pricing a lever whose shipped nonce is dirty.
- `pkill -f <pattern>` matches the killing script's own command line if the pattern appears in it.
  Cost me one chained job this session.
